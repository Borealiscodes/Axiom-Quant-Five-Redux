\section{Spectral Operator \texorpdfstring{$\Phi_5$}{Phi5}}
\label{sec:spectral}

This appendix provides the full mathematical development of the spectral
operator $\Phi_5$, corresponding to altitude $A5$ and domain $D_2$ in the
AxiomQuant Five Redux system. The operator acts on the soft 80D Riemannian
manifold $(\mathcal{M}^{80}, g)$ defined in Appendix~\ref{sec:manifold}.

\subsection{Spectral Domain}

The spectral domain $D_2$ consists of all points $x \in \mathcal{M}$ equipped
with the metric $g$ and its induced Laplace--Beltrami operator. Formally:
\[
D_2 = \{ x \in \mathcal{M} \mid g(x) \text{ is defined and smooth} \}.
\]

Altitude alignment:
\[
A5 \leftrightarrow D_2.
\]

\subsection{Laplace--Beltrami Operator}

Define the Laplace--Beltrami operator acting on smooth scalar fields $f$:
\[
\Delta f = \nabla^i \nabla_i f,
\]
where $\nabla$ is the Levi-Civita connection associated with $g$.

In coordinates:
\[
\Delta f = \frac{1}{\sqrt{|g|}}
\partial_i \left( \sqrt{|g|} g^{ij} \partial_j f \right).
\]

Smoothness assumption:
\[
\Delta : C^\infty(\mathcal{M}) \to C^\infty(\mathcal{M}).
\]

\subsection{Spectral Decomposition}

The operator $\Delta$ admits a spectral decomposition:
\[
\Delta \phi_k = \lambda_k \phi_k,
\]
where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $\lambda_k \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ are eigenvalues,
    \item $\phi_k \in C^\infty(\mathcal{M})$ are eigenfunctions,
    \item $\{\phi_k\}$ forms an orthonormal basis of $L^2(\mathcal{M})$.
\end{itemize}

We assume the spectrum is discrete, which holds for compact $\mathcal{M}$.

\subsection{Spectral Projection}

Given $x \in \mathcal{M}$, define the spectral projection:
\[
s(x) = \Phi_5(x)
= \left( \phi_1(x), \phi_2(x), \dots, \phi_m(x) \right),
\]
where $m$ is a fixed spectral bandwidth.

This produces a vector in $\mathbb{R}^m$ encoding the spectral signature of $x$.

\subsection{Operator Definition}

Formally:
\[
\Phi_5 : \mathcal{M} \to \mathbb{R}^m,
\qquad
\Phi_5(x) = (\phi_1(x), \dots, \phi_m(x)).
\]

Altitude alignment:
\[
\Phi_5 : A3 \to A5.
\]

Domain alignment:
\[
\Phi_5 : D_1 \to D_2.
\]

\subsection{Smoothness}

Since each $\phi_k$ is smooth:
\[
\Phi_5 \in C^\infty(\mathcal{M}, \mathbb{R}^m).
\]

\subsection{Spectral Stability}

For sufficiently small perturbations of the metric:
\[
\| \Delta g \| < \varepsilon
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\| \Delta \Phi_5(x) \| < C \varepsilon.
\]

This follows from standard perturbation theory for elliptic operators.

\subsection{Spectral Reversibility}

On the spectral domain $D_2$, the map $\Phi_5$ is injective up to isospectral
symmetry:
\[
\Phi_5(x) = \Phi_5(x') \Rightarrow x \sim x'.
\]

This equivalence is resolved by higher-altitude operators.

\subsection{Spectral Monotonicity}

Altitude monotonicity:
\[
x \le x' \Rightarrow \Phi_5(x) \le \Phi_5(x').
\]

This follows from the ordering of eigenvalues under metric expansion.

\subsection{Operator Role}

The spectral operator provides the first geometric lift in the Redux pipeline:
\[
x \mapsto s(x),
\]
feeding directly into the curvature operator $\Phi_7$.

This completes the formal development of the spectral operator.
