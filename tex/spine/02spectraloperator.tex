\section{Spectral Operator and Eigenbasis Construction}

\subsection{Overview}

The spectral operator defines the transition from altitude A5 to A7 in
the Redux geometric pipeline. Its purpose is to extract a stable
eigenbasis from the manifold's metric structure and project input
vectors into this basis. This section formalizes the spectral
decomposition and establishes the mathematical properties required for
later curvature and holonomy modulation.

\subsection{Spectral Decomposition}

Given the metric tensor $g_x$ at a point $x \in M$, we define the
associated symmetric matrix
\[
G(x) \in \mathbb{R}^{80 \times 80}.
\]
Because $G(x)$ is positive-definite, it admits an eigendecomposition
\[
G(x) = U(x) \Lambda(x) U(x)^\top,
\]
where
\begin{itemize}
    \item $U(x)$ is an orthonormal matrix of eigenvectors,
    \item $\Lambda(x)$ is a diagonal matrix of eigenvalues
          $\lambda_1(x), \ldots, \lambda_{80}(x)$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Projection Operator}

For any vector $v \in \mathbb{R}^{80}$, the spectral projection is
defined as
\[
s = U(x)^\top v.
\]
This representation expresses $v$ in the eigenbasis of the local
geometry. The projection is stable under small perturbations of $G(x)$
due to the smooth dependence of eigenvectors on the metric.

\subsection{Stability Bounds}

Let $\delta G$ be a perturbation of the metric tensor. Standard
perturbation theory yields
\[
\| U(x + \delta x) - U(x) \| \leq C \| \delta G \|,
\]
for some constant $C$ depending on the spectral gap of $G(x)$. This
ensures that the spectral operator behaves smoothly across the manifold
and does not introduce discontinuities into the embedding pipeline.

\subsection{Altitude Interpretation}

The spectral operator lifts the representation from altitude A5
(normalized structural features) to altitude A7 (spectral geometry).
This transition is essential for capturing global geometric behavior
that cannot be expressed through local degree-based features alone.

\subsection{Output}

The output of the spectral operator is the projected vector
\[
s \in \mathbb{R}^{80},
\]
which serves as the input to the curvature envelope operator in the
next stage of the pipeline.
