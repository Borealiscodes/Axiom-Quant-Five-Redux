\section{The 80D Soft Manifold Structure}

\subsection{Definition}

We define the ambient geometric space of the Redux runtime as an
$80$-dimensional soft manifold
\[
M = (\mathbb{R}^{80}, g),
\]
equipped with a smoothly varying metric tensor $g$ that encodes the
local geometric behavior of the embedding pipeline. The manifold is
not intended to model a physical geometry; rather, it provides a
stable mathematical substrate for spectral, curvature, holonomy, and
topological operators.

\subsection{Coordinate Charts}

Let $\{U_i\}_{i=1}^{k}$ be an open cover of $\mathbb{R}^{80}$ such that
each $U_i$ admits a coordinate chart
\[
\varphi_i : U_i \to \mathbb{R}^{80}.
\]
Because the manifold is modeled directly on $\mathbb{R}^{80}$, the
charts are globally compatible, and transition maps are smooth.

\subsection{Metric Tensor}

The metric tensor $g$ is defined pointwise by
\[
g_x(u, v) = u^\top G(x) v,
\]
where $G(x)$ is a positive-definite matrix depending smoothly on $x$.
This allows the manifold to encode curvature envelopes and holonomy
transport in later sections.

\subsection{Curvature}

The Riemann curvature tensor is defined in the usual way:
\[
R(u, v)w = \nabla_u \nabla_v w - \nabla_v \nabla_u w - \nabla_{[u,v]} w.
\]
In practice, the curvature envelope operator extracts a normalized
curvature signature
\[
\kappa(x) \in \mathbb{R}^{80},
\]
which is used to modulate spectral projections.

\subsection{Manifold as Embedding Domain}

The manifold $M$ serves as the target space for embeddings of
Erdős–Rényi graphs and other symbolic structures. Given a graph $G$,
the embedding pipeline produces a vector
\[
e(G) \in M,
\]
whose coordinates encode spectral, curvature, holonomy, and topological
features of the input structure.
