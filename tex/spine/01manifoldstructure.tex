\section{Soft 80D Riemannian Manifold}
\label{sec:manifold}

This appendix provides the full geometric development of the soft
80-dimensional Riemannian manifold used throughout the AxiomQuant Five Redux
system. All operators $\Phi_5, \Phi_7, \Phi_8, \Phi_8^t, \Phi_9$ act on this
manifold, and all altitude and domain transitions are defined relative to its
geometric structure.

\subsection{Manifold Definition}

Let $\mathcal{M}$ be a smooth manifold of dimension $80$. We assume:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item $\mathcal{M}$ is Hausdorff and second-countable.
    \item $\mathcal{M}$ admits a smooth atlas $\{(U_\alpha, \varphi_\alpha)\}$.
    \item All transition maps are $C^\infty$.
\end{enumerate}

We write $\mathcal{M}^{80}$ when emphasizing the dimension.

\subsection{Metric Tensor}

A smooth Riemannian metric
\[
g : T\mathcal{M} \times T\mathcal{M} \to \mathbb{R}
\]
is assumed. In coordinates $(x^1,\dots,x^{80})$,
\[
g = g_{ij}(x)\, dx^i \otimes dx^j,
\]
with $g_{ij}(x)$ symmetric and positive-definite.

Smoothness assumption:
\[
g_{ij} \in C^\infty(\mathcal{M}).
\]

\subsection{Levi-Civita Connection}

The Levi-Civita connection $\nabla$ is uniquely determined by $g$ via:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Metric compatibility: $\nabla g = 0$.
    \item Torsion-free: $T(X,Y) = \nabla_X Y - \nabla_Y X - [X,Y] = 0$.
\end{enumerate}

Connection coefficients:
\[
\Gamma^k_{\ ij}
= \frac{1}{2} g^{k\ell}
\left(
\partial_i g_{\ell j}
+ \partial_j g_{\ell i}
- \partial_\ell g_{ij}
\right).
\]

\subsection{Curvature Tensor}

The Riemann curvature tensor is defined by:
\[
R(X,Y)Z = \nabla_X \nabla_Y Z - \nabla_Y \nabla_X Z - \nabla_{[X,Y]} Z.
\]

In coordinates:
\[
R^i_{\ jkl}
= \partial_k \Gamma^i_{\ jl}
- \partial_l \Gamma^i_{\ jk}
+ \Gamma^i_{\ km}\Gamma^m_{\ jl}
- \Gamma^i_{\ lm}\Gamma^m_{\ jk}.
\]

Smoothness assumption:
\[
R^i_{\ jkl} \in C^\infty(\mathcal{M}).
\]

\subsection{Holonomy Field}

Define the holonomy field
\[
H : \mathcal{M} \to \mathrm{SO}(80)
\]
as the smooth assignment of parallel-transport operators around infinitesimal
loops.

Formally:
\[
H(x) = \exp\!\left( \int_{\gamma_x} R \right),
\]
where $\gamma_x$ is an infinitesimal loop based at $x$.

Smoothness assumption:
\[
H \in C^\infty(\mathcal{M}, \mathrm{SO}(80)).
\]

\subsection{Topology Signature}

Define the topology signature
\[
\tau : \mathcal{M} \to \mathbb{R}^{80}
\]
as a smooth field encoding local topological variation.

Examples include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Betti-number gradients,
    \item local homology shifts,
    \item micro-fibration signatures.
\end{itemize}

Smoothness assumption:
\[
\tau \in C^\infty(\mathcal{M}, \mathbb{R}^{80}).
\]

\subsection{Soft-Manifold Constraint}

The manifold is \emph{soft} in the sense that small perturbations of the metric
do not induce topological collapse.

Formally, for sufficiently small $\varepsilon > 0$:
\[
\| \Delta g \| < \varepsilon
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Delta \tau \text{ is bounded}.
\]

This ensures that all operators $\Phi_5, \Phi_7, \Phi_8, \Phi_8^t, \Phi_9$ are
well-defined and smooth.

\subsection{Operator-Ready Structure}

The manifold provides the geometric primitives required by the operator chain:

\begin{itemize}
    \item $\Phi_5$ uses $g$ for spectral projection.
    \item $\Phi_7$ uses $R$ for curvature modulation.
    \item $\Phi_8$ uses $H$ for holonomy transport.
    \item $\Phi_8^t$ uses $\tau$ for topology modulation.
    \item $\Phi_9$ uses all fields to extract invariants.
\end{itemize}

This completes the formal manifold structure required by the canonical spine.
